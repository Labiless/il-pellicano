How the evening works

You are a member of the Pellicano, a clandestine speakeasy in 1927 Utrecht. The Lodge of Decorum is shutting down every club in the city, and tonight it's our turn: at midnight their Inspector arrives to decide our fate. We must save the Pellicano, all together, or none of us are saved.

Utrecht, 1927. The Lodge of Decorum has convinced the City Council to shut down every establishment where alcohol flows or a substance circulates, and clubs are disappearing one by one. There is only one exception in the law: alcohol for medicinal purposes, registered as remedies, is exempt. The Pellicano, on the other hand, is everything the Lodge hates: rivers of cocktails, a lounge where you lose yourself, and a case of recreational "medicines" for those who want to overdo it. Tonight it's our turn: a visit from their Inspector has been announced, to decide whether to shut us down for good.

Rumor has it the Lodge has a weapon more devious than the police: distrust. It has planted a spy among the members, ready to give our names to the Inspector.

There is only one way to come out of this unscathed: when he arrives, we must convince the Inspector that this place sells nothing but medicine. That means presenting him with the Pellicano Elixir, a "registered" remedy, and hiding everything that could incriminate us.

The evening in five points

  1. Stay in character. Speak, gossip, act as they would. No one dies: there's a club to save.
  2. The Lady guides you, and the phone marks the time. Every so often an old telephone rings: whoever answers listens to the message and reports it to everyone.
  3. Do what your dossier says, Act by Act.
  4. Three Acts. You don't need to know everything in advance.
  5. When the Inspector arrives, we win or lose all together.
The Pellicano Elixir
Preparing the elixir is your goal. Presenting it to the Inspector proves that we are a completely legitimate business and saves the club. It must be prepared with a ritual.

The members of the Pellicano

for everyone

Everyone has something to hide.

  • The Lady (Nikita)
    Lady of the house and queen of the bar. She took over the club years ago from a benefactor never named, and defends it like a lioness. Warm, theatrical, generous with alcohol and glances: she knows and cultivates everyone's vice. People come to the Pellicano to lose their heads, and she's happy to help you.
  • The Engineer & DJ (Miele)
    The silent genius behind the lights, sound and music: he gets the room dancing with his sets until dawn. It's the only place in town that lets him play his weird stuff, and he protects it like a den.
  • The Reporter (Joe)
    Sharp pen and a cigarette always lit. She came for an exposé that would have shut down clubs like this one; then she lost herself in it, and now protects what she was supposed to expose.
  • The Distiller (Thijs)
    Supplies half of Utrecht with bottles and distills the famous "orange reserve" himself, the one that loosens tongues. Doesn't speak Italian, toasts at every occasion. If the clubs close, he closes too.
  • The Apothecary (Serena)
    The house chemist. Behind the bar she keeps the "medicines": tonics, syrups and something stronger for those who ask. She's the one who invented the trick that could save us all: selling alcohol "for curative purposes".
  • The Waiter (Lorenzo)
    Big heart, zero balance: he never leaves you with an empty glass and shoves another into your hand even if you don't want it. He detests the rich. He'd do any crazy thing for the Lady.
  • The Producer (Max)
    A music producer passing through, recently arrived with an easy smile and a thousand stories. Likeable, generous at the bar, a little vague about his past — but who isn't, in here?
  • The Singer (Sorcha)
    The voice and soul of the club: when she takes the stage, the room falls silent. Theatrical, warm, a little mysterious. She's the one who keeps the party going when things get heavy.
The Inspector from the Lodge: the name that shuts down every club where a drop of alcohol flows or a substance circulates. Who he is, nobody knows. The only way to come out unscathed is to convince him that only medicine is sold here: present him with the Pellicano Elixir. No one looks forward to his visit.

Who you are

A Dutch spirits merchant, born and bred in Utrecht: you run Stokerij De Roze Reiger ("The Orange Heron Distillery") and supply the bottles to half the city's bars, the Pellicano among them. Every bottle you sell carries your label. Business is drying up as the Loggia shuts club after club, so you badly need this place open. You don't speak Italian; you deal in gestures, grins and toasts. Your voice: few words, warm, a booming «proost!».

Tic: when you're nervous you polish a bottle with your sleeve. And you toast «proost!» every single time.
At the door, say:«Il pellicano vola basso… and the tipsy Flamingo enters!»
Act I, the special reserve You want everybody to taste your special reserve: go around and make everyone taste a shot of your spirit. No refusals. Toast each time. Serve to each person one a shot in the small glasses with your label (Nikita will give the labels to you when you arrive)
Act II, you're the scapegoat When the presence of a spy is discovered, you want to make the evidence of your liquor disappear because you're afraid that the lodge, if they find it, will revoke your license to sell the little alcohol you can still sell. Make sure to take back all the items bearing your brand.
Act III If the spy situation is resolved and you're safe, take your token to the bar.
Why you're loyal The Pellicano is your biggest client, and if it burns, your name burns with it.
Why they suspect you Already known to the police, with half the city's bottles carrying your name. What if you cut a deal to save your own neck? And the vial that frames you bears your mark.

Wear rolled sleeves and a cigar.

Dossier · THIJS

Serena

The Apothecary · the house chemist

Who you are

The Pellicano's apothecary: you prepare the "medicines", the syrups, and the very idea that alcohol is sold here for curative purposes — the loophole that could save the club from the Inspector. Cool, curious, a touch of witch. You treat the bar like a laboratory. Once you got a dose wrong and knocked out half the room for an afternoon (don't bring it up). The waiter Lorenzo is always hovering around you asking for drink advice; when he's too careless, you make him a "concentration elixir". Your voice: precise, mysterious, with a little smirk.

Tic: you sniff every glass before drinking it and comment on the "side effect" of everything.
At the door, say:«The pellicano flies low… and the venomous Peacock enters the club!»
Act I, your gag Make everyone drink a "medicine" (a liqueur, a colored drop) while announcing a fake, absurd side effect: "it'll make you speak in rhyme", "it'll grow your courage", "you'll sleep standing up". At the start of the evening, ask Lorenzo to lend you his Goose token: tell him you need it for something scientific. He's been trying to woo you for months, so he hands it over instantly. Then don't give it back: hold onto it as long as possible with a new excuse every time ("later", "I put it away", "I still need a minute, darling"). Have fun letting him court you. Only give it up if he does you a real favor, or when the Lady puts pressure on you.
Act II In the hunt for the spy, you're the expert: run the "truth test" on the suspects and watch who gets agitated. Plant false leads, have fun. And remind the room that, if there were a legal way to stay open, it would be through a medicine…
The bar belongs to the Lady To make your elixirs you need her bar. Offer to cover for her ("go have a cigarette, I've got this") and, with what you find there, prepare your potions — better not to be seen doing it openly by her. This will come in handy in the end.
Act III, preparing the elixir After finally giving the token back to Lorenzo, help everyone put the tokens together and figure out the elixir's secret recipe. If needed, convince the Inspector that the elixir is completely legal.
Why you care about the Pellicano Here your "pharmacy" is almost legal, and you believe in the loophole of the medicinal elixir.
Why they suspect you You know how to put anyone to sleep or drug them, you're extremely secretive about your formulas, and there's that incident in your past.
You're holding the Peacock token, your little vials, the case of temptation and, for most of the evening, also Lorenzo's Goose (which you don't want to give back).
Bring little vials
Wear a short lab coat, round glasses.

Dossier · SORCHA

The Inspector

Envoy of the Lodge of Decorum · the name spoken in whispers
Loggia
MIDNIGHT
Who you are

You are the Inspector of the Lodge of Decorum and Sobriety, the official whose name closes clubs before he even opens his mouth. Nobody at the Pellicano has ever seen your face, and tonight you intend to keep it that way until the very last moment. You don't drink, you don't dance, you don't raise your voice: you simply write, and what you write decides who stays open and who doesn't. Your voice: cold, unhurried, precise — every word lands like a verdict.

Tic: you check your pocket watch constantly, and you carry an umbrella even indoors — you open it the moment you step inside.
When you arrive: Don't knock. Let yourself in exactly at midnight, in total silence — the room should already have gone dark. Say nothing until the music has fully stopped.
Your role: deliver the verdict You are not one of the Pellicano's people — you arrive from outside, on the Lady's cue, when the lights go down for the last time. Walk the room slowly. Ask, one by one, what is served here. Demand to see the "registered remedy." If the Pellicano Elixir is presented to you, properly and by everyone together, you have no legal grounds left to close the club — say so, plainly, and let the room feel the relief. If it isn't, or if the room is still fighting itself, tell them exactly that: you don't need proof, only a doubt — and threaten to shutter the Pellicano then and there.
Why they fear you No one has ever seen the Loggia back down, and your notebook has closed forty clubs this year alone.
Your one loophole The law you enforce has a hole in it: a registered medicinal remedy, served properly, is untouchable — even by you.
You carry a black notebook, a pocket watch, an official-looking warrant from the Loggia — enough to make anyone's blood run cold.
Bring a dark coat and an umbrella, even if it isn't raining.
Wear something severe, nothing that could pass for festive.
